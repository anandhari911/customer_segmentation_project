# customer_segmentation_project

The objective is to

1.)Identify  Customer Behaviour Pattern based on how they recently, how they often buy and how much they spend.
2.)Segment the Customer Score i.e. to categorize the customers into 5 distinctive non overlapping groups (Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, Lost and New Customers) using clear 1-5 scoring rules.
3.) Estimate each segment's exact share of overall customers and total revenue to pinpoint where business value and churn risks lie
4.) Provide clear, targeted recommendations for each segment(such as VIP rewards for Champions, win-back campaigns for At Risk customers, or welcome series for New Customers) to maximize customer lifetime value and marketing ROI.

the main objective of this project is to segment a customer base using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis to help business leadership—specifically the Marketing Manager—move away from treating all customers the same.
